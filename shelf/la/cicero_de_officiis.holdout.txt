Sulla;
Lucius Cornelius Sulla Felix (138-78), noble, profligate, brilliant genius; would stoop to anything, I, 109; soldier against Jugurtha, Mithradates, Marius, Rome; statesman; reformed the constitution; absolute monarch of Rome (81-79); treatment of tributary allies, III, 87; confiscator, I, 43; II, 29; overturned the old morals, II, 27; Cicero opposed him, II, 51.
