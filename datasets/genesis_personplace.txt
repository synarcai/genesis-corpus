where in the word «קורא» does the person stand? NOT IN THE WORD: THE TAIL HOLDS NUMBER, NOT PERSON: the stem «קורא» with no tail. That is why «הוא קורא» is he reads and «אנחנו קוראים» is we read.
where in the word «படிக்கிறாய்» does the person stand? IN THE TAIL OF THE WORD: the stem «படிக்கிற» and the tail «ாய்». That is why «நீ படிக்கிறாய்» is you read and «நான் படிக்கிறேன்» is I read.
where in the word «ያነባል» does the person stand? AT BOTH ENDS OF THE WORD: the head «ያ» and the stem «ነባ», then the tail «ል». That is why «እሱ ያነባል» is he reads and «እኔ አነባለሁ» is I read.
where in the word «läser» does the person stand? NOWHERE: one word «läser», with no head and no tail. That is why «jag läser» is I read and «du läser» is you read.
where in the word «כותב» does the person stand? NOT IN THE WORD: THE TAIL HOLDS NUMBER, NOT PERSON: the stem «כותב» with no tail. That is why «אתה כותב» is you write and «אנחנו כותבים» is we write.
where in the word «διαβάζω» does the person stand? IN THE TAIL OF THE WORD: the stem «διαβάζ» and the tail «ω». That is why «εγώ διαβάζω» is I read and «εμείς διαβάζουμε» is we read.
where in the word «أكتب» does the person stand? IN THE HEAD OF THE WORD: the head «أ» and the stem «كتب», and no tail at all. That is why «أنا أكتب» is I write and «أنت تكتب» is you write.
