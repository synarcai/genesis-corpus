London is 2 hours behind Cairo. it is noon in Cairo — and already 10 in London: 12 − 2 = 10. NOON IS NOON ONLY WHERE IT IS NOON.
Moscow and Istanbul stand in one zone, +3: the difference between them is 0 hours, and when it is 18 in Moscow it is 18 in Istanbul as well: 18 − 0 = 18. A TIME ZONE IS PRECISELY THE SET OF PLACES WHERE THE HOUR IS ONE AND THE SAME.

London is 9 hours behind Tokyo. a day has 24 hours. when it is 5 in Tokyo, it is 20 in London on the PREVIOUS day: 5 − 9 = −4, and −4 + 24 = 20. THE DAY IS CLOSED INTO A CIRCLE, AND GOING PAST ITS START YOU RETURN TO THE END OF THE ONE BEFORE.
Tokyo is 15 hours ahead of Chicago. a day has 24 hours. when it is 20 in Chicago, it is 11 in Tokyo on the NEXT day: 20 + 15 = 35, and 35 − 24 = 11. THE SAME CIRCLE WALKED THE OTHER WAY.
