a bag holds 4 balls: 4 red and 0 blue. «NOT ALL are red» is false, for the red ones are 4 and the balls are 4. «ALL are NOT red» is false, for the red ones are 4 and would have to be 0. Here both are false and so seem to be one; but that is only the CASE of a full bag.
