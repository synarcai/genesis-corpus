which form stands after «viisi»? The partitive «taloa»: «viisi taloa» is five houses. With «yksi» it is the nominative «talo», and the plural «talot» does not stand after a number at all.
which form stands after «kolme»? The partitive «kirjaa»: «kolme kirjaa» is three books. With «yksi» it is the nominative «kirja», and the plural «kirjat» does not stand after a number at all.
which form stands after «kaksi»? The partitive «katua»: «kaksi katua» is two streets. With «yksi» it is the nominative «katu», and the plural «kadut» does not stand after a number at all.
