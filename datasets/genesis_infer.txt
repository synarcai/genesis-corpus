if a number is divisible by 6, it is divisible by 3. such a statement is called an IMPLICATION: the first half is the ANTECEDENT, the second the CONSEQUENT.
what follows from 40 being divisible by 8? 40 is divisible by 8. so 40 is divisible by 4. this step is called AFFIRMING — modus ponens.
