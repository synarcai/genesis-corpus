how does one say «flowers» in Indonesian? One says «bunga-bunga» — by repeating the word «bunga» (a flower). And with a numeral it is «dua bunga», where the repetition would be redundant.
how does one say «mountains» in Indonesian? One says «gunung-gunung» — by repeating the word «gunung» (a mountain). And with a numeral it is «dua gunung», where the repetition would be redundant.
