2 cm² is NOT 20 mm² but 200 mm²: the length factor 10 applied to an area is short by exactly 10 times, for 20 × 10 = 200. A LENGTH FACTOR PUT UPON AN AREA UNDERSTATES IT BY AS MUCH AS THE FACTOR ITSELF.
3 km = 3000 m, and the factor here is PLAIN — 1000, not 1000000. IT IS RIGHT ONLY BECAUSE THE POWER IS ONE: the same unit squared would take 1000000.
