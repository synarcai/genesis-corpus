a box holds 17 balls: 10 red, 9 large, and 7 red among the large. given «the ball is large», «the ball is red» gives 7 out of 9, while with no condition it gives 10 out of 17. KNOWLEDGE NARROWS THE SET OF OUTCOMES, IT DOES NOT CHANGE THE THING.
a box holds 20 balls: 12 red, 5 large, and 3 red among the large. given «the ball is large», «the ball is red» gives 3 out of 5 — the same share as 12 out of 20, for 3 × 20 = 12 × 5. KNOWLEDGE THAT DOES NOT NARROW THE SHARE DOES NOT CHANGE THE PROBABILITY.
