«один дім» (one house). It is not the number of things that decides but THE LAST DIGIT: «двадцять один дім», «двадцять два доми», «двадцять п'ять домів». THE CELL IS READ OFF THE TAIL OF THE NUMBER, NOT ITS SIZE.

Ukrainian says «два книги», Russian «два книги». The words are alike, the cells are built DIFFERENTLY: the Russian second is a GENITIVE SINGULAR, the Ukrainian one a NOMINATIVE PLURAL. TWO CLOSE LANGUAGES THAT PART IN ONE CELL ARE MORE DANGEROUS THAN TWO DISTANT ONES.
«п'ять столів», «шість столів», «дванадцять столів» — the third form is taken by every number from five to twenty. IT IS RARE IN THE SHOWING, NOT IN THE LANGUAGE.
