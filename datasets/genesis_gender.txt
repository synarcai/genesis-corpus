der Mond (мужского), die Katze (женского), das Fenster (среднего) — три разных рода; а во множественном у всех трёх артикль ОДИН: die Monde, die Katzen, die Fenster. Множественное число РОДА НЕ РАЗЛИЧАЕТ.

«дом» по-немецки das Haus (среднего рода), по-французски la maison (женского), по-испански la casa (женского), по-русски дом (мужского). Предмет ОДИН, а родов 3: род живёт в СЛОВЕ, а не в предмете.

die Tür — «door» in German: the word Tür is feminine, and that is why the article is die. GENDER is a property of the WORD, and the article is its sign.
der Hund — «dog» in German: the word Hund is masculine, and that is why the article is der. GENDER is a property of the WORD, and the article is its sign.
la fenêtre — «window» in French: the word fenêtre is feminine, and that is why the article is la. GENDER is a property of the WORD, and the article is its sign.
la fleur — «flower» in French: the word fleur is feminine, and that is why the article is la. GENDER is a property of the WORD, and the article is its sign.
la luna — «moon» in Spanish: the word luna is feminine, and that is why the article is la. GENDER is a property of the WORD, and the article is its sign.
das Weib means «woman», and the gender of Weib is neuter: THE GENDER OF A WORD IS NOT THE SEX OF THE THING. That is why one says «das Weib» and not otherwise.

the word itself does not show the gender: Hund, Sonne, Haus end differently and their genders differ. The articles speak: der Hund (masculine), die Sonne (feminine), das Haus (neuter). That is why the article is learnt TOGETHER with the word.
der Mond (masculine), die Katze (feminine), das Fenster (neuter) — three different genders; and in the plural all three take ONE article: die Monde, die Katzen, die Fenster. The plural DOES NOT DISTINGUISH GENDER.

«house» is das Haus in German (neuter), la maison in French (feminine), la casa in Spanish (feminine), and дом in Russian (masculine). The thing is ONE, and the genders are 3: gender lives in the WORD, not in the thing.
