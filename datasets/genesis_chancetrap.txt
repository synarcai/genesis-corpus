three sacks hold 4, 5 and 18 coins: 4 + 5 + 18 = 27, 27 ÷ 3 = 9. The average is 9, and NOT ONE of them holds 9: they hold 4, 5 and 18. AN AVERAGE IS A NUMBER OF THE SET, NOT A NUMBER OF A MEMBER.
three baskets hold 2, 3 and 10 apples: 2 + 3 + 10 = 15, 15 ÷ 3 = 5. The average is 5, and NOT ONE of them holds 5: they hold 2, 3 and 10. AN AVERAGE IS A NUMBER OF THE SET, NOT A NUMBER OF A MEMBER.
2, 6, 16 give the average 8: 2 + 6 + 16 = 24, 24 ÷ 3 = 8. And 8, 8, 8 give the very same: 8 × 3 = 24. The sets are DIFFERENT — the first has a spread of 14 (16 − 2), the second 0 — and the average is one. AN AVERAGE SAYS NOTHING ABOUT THE SPREAD.
